Add your background image to this folder and name it exactly:

    background.jpg

(same folder as HealthLens.fxml and styles.css)

The app looks for /com/healthlens/background.jpg on the classpath. If it's not
found, HealthLens still runs fine — it just falls back to a plain gradient
background defined in styles.css.

If you'd rather use a .png, either:
  1) rename your file to background.jpg (JavaFX's Image loader doesn't care
     about the true format, just make sure it's a valid image file), or
  2) open HealthLensController.java and change:
         getClass().getResourceAsStream("background.jpg")
     to:
         getClass().getResourceAsStream("background.png")

Delete this .txt file once you've added your real image — it won't affect
the build if you leave it, it's just a reminder.
